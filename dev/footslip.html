Foot-slip rig

A CPU mirror of the quadruped leg math (src/render/models/legmath.ts, which the vertex shader is ported from) is driven along a path the way AnimalLayer drives the shader. Slip = planted-foot motion in the world during stance / body motion over the same frames. Before = the pre-R1 leg math, after = the exact foot lock with ground-reading IK. The same numbers print from npx tsx dev/footslip.ts.
