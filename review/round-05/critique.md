# Round 5 blind critique (fresh critic, folder path only, 2026-10-03 23:10)

Scores: Visual quality 6 / Readability 4 / Sense of life and motion 5 / Ecosystem believability 5 / UI clarity 7 / Would I keep watching 5.

Top issues (summarized; full text in the session transcript):
1. Critical: predators and predation can't be seen at the default zoom; wolves are slivers; the kill reads like a dropped prop. Scale with distance, contrast, and an auto-director that cuts to chases and kills.
2. Critical: dynamics read as noise; wolves flat 40 to 80; ravens pinned at about 320; prey swing too fast for their breeding; in-game herds grow between late summer and early winter.
3. Major: the first hour is a decline (bison about 1,100 to about 400 in the first decade).
4. Major: seasons unreadable: white patches in spring and summer; winter snow reads as sand; moonlit grass reads as snow; spring green is a saturated lawn green.
5. Major: distant land looks like a desert.
6. Major: evolution hits a ceiling or floor and stops.
7. Major: herds move like an algorithm (donuts, arcs, dense clumps, a stampede ending in the pond).
8. Major: one drought can wipe out a species for good (wolves 16 of 20 after a drought).
9. Minor: artifacts: burnt-looking trees, a glowing white smear in a herd (pronghorn at dusk), red bison, overlapping wolf meshes.
10. Minor: chart and HUD tidiness: stacked graph labels collide, flat sparklines, chronicle cut mid-sentence, raven labels unreadable in dev charts.

What's working: the light and mood; pronghorn design; the chronicle and title cards; the inspector; the system holds over 700 years and the wolf-wipe test shows real predator control; the territory overlay.

Single change that would help most: make the wolves and their hunts impossible to miss, with distance-scaled, high-contrast predators and an auto-director camera that cuts to every chase and kill.
