# Round 15 blind critique (v7c default world, 2026-10-07)

Fresh blind-critic agent, given only this folder (11 stills of the live v7c build, plus population and phase charts from the v7c 20-seed acceptance).

**Scores:** visual 6, readability 4, life and motion 5, ecosystem believability 5, UI clarity 6, keep watching 5.

**First impression:** handsome, moody prairie with strong light, but every wide shot is a brown carpet of over a thousand bison.

**Issues, ranked by the critic:**
1. Critical: bison carpet. One continuous swarm in 01, 06, 07, 09; a ring in 06; ant-trail lines from the top (09). Counts climb from 836 to 1,229 in four years. Fewer, distinct, well-spaced herds with structure.
2. Critical: long-run dynamics look like fast jitter, not multi-year boom and crash arcs; the phase portraits are tangles without rotation.
3. Major: mule deer crash to about 10 and stay there for centuries; prairie dog towns of about 20; ravens hit a flat ceiling near 680.
4. Major: small animals invisible (pronghorn are flecks, no visible carcass in the ravens shot, an alarm field note points at a smudge, jackrabbits never seen).
5. Major: the first in-game decades are a warm-up (wolves take about 70 years to settle, deer collapse later).
6. Major: wide-view terrain looks blotchy (snow camouflage patches), river a flat ribbon, haze washes out mid distance, square map edge visible from the top.
7. Major: the territory overlay legend promises tinted ranges that do not show; only two labels.
8. Minor: chronicle repeats lines word for word (coyote arrivals, disease with the same count).
9. Minor: UI footprint (15 tools plus 11 sparklines), rail reflows when the graph expands, unlabeled graph dots, per-sparkline scales exaggerate small changes.
10. Minor: winter trees all the same dead snag; a grey creature floating near the camera; bison clipping and tilt.

**Working:** lighting and time of day, wolves reading against gold grass, the follow panel and family tree, the chronicle voice and title cards, robustness (10 of 11 species persist in 20 of 20 seeds), the unattended-viewing touches.

**Single biggest change:** break the bison carpet into a manageable number of distinct, well-spaced herds.
