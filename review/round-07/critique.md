## Top issues
1. **Critical: the predators are flat lines.** Wolves about 50 and ravens about 300 for over a millennium in every seed; a hard flat raven top near 320 reads as a ceiling. In the close-up, prey swing 40% to 230% while wolves stay within 15% and never lag. In the drought, bison nearly vanish while wolves dip a few animals. Fix: wolf recruitment and survival tied to kill rate; ravens tied to carrion.
2. **Critical: one world in twenty dies** (seed 11, with grass at its highest), and 17 to 19 of 20 persist in the shocks. Fix: a low-density safety net (immigration, a refuge herd).
3. **Major: evolution runs to a cap and stops.** Bison speed, size and litter and pronghorn litter shoot up for 200 to 300 years then flatten; both litters plateau at about 2.38, which looks clamped; bison herding collapses from 1.0 to 0.15 and sight to 0.65. The last century of several panels jumps (sample thinning). Fix: real trait costs; truncate the chart where few worlds remain.
4. **Major: the seasons run backwards in the counters.** Bison climb from 835 to 955 and pronghorn from 383 to 490 between early autumn and late winter, then bison fall in mid-spring; wolves and ravens sit at exactly 30 all year. Fix: a spring birth pulse and winter attrition.
5. **Major: water and night don't read.** The river is a flat grey stripe like a paved highway in 01, 09 and 11 and a lavender ribbon in 06; at night (07) the land is a wavy teal surface like a stormy ocean with the grass texture gone; the pond in 08 has big dark blotches like cow print.
6. **Major: weather and season labels lie.** "Rain" in the header with crisp sun and hard shadows; bare snags in early winter (05, 06) but full green trees by the pond in late winter (07); no snow or frost in winter.
7. **Major: the Chronicle is too thin.** The same three Year 1 entries sit from autumn to spring; about 7 entries by year 5; "The Bluff pack is no more" while the wolf count rises; nothing on the first winter, pups, or ravens quadrupling.
8. **Major: herds turn into texture at wide zoom.** A dark carpet (01, 07), rust leaf litter (06), sawdust (09); wolves from above look like crumpled paper; in 03 the wolves at the bottom edge are tall pale shapes like fish standing on their tails.
9. **Minor: the follow panel contradicts itself:** "Alpha female wolf, 6 years, one of the first wolves" with 0 offspring and 0 kills.
10. **Minor: UI and chart rough edges:** the expanded graph covers a third of the scene; tool buttons hide the Hollow pack label; "100x: this machine is running at 20x"; in-game grass cover 0.15 to 0.3 against a lush screen; the 20-world chart legend cites an undrawn right axis; persist labels over the raven data; beige blur at the top of 04, 09, 11; a mown-lawn edge in 04.

## What's working
1. Golden-hour and dusk lighting on the tallgrass is wallpaper-grade; the low dusk shot of bare trees over blue-grey grass is the best image in the set.
2. Wolves in cool blue-grey pop against amber grass; the follow panel (traits against founder and species ticks, "Ride along on a hunt") is a strong documentary hook.
3. The territory overlay is excellent: named packs, headcounts, clean rings and dens.
4. The Chronicle's voice and the serif type on dark glass fit the documentary tone.
5. The shock tests tell a real trophic story: wolves removed release the grazers, which settle when wolves return; the drought crash recovers in about 20 years.

**Single change that would help most:** make wolves live and die by what they kill, and ravens by the carrion available, so the predator lines lag and chase the prey booms.
