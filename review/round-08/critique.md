# Round 8 blind critique (stills 01 to 11, charts 12 to 15)

Fresh blind-critic subagent, given only this folder path. Build dcf4809 (deployed 09:01), captured about 09:05 from a clean worktree. Charts from the 2x calendar acceptance and merged shock suite, redrawn with the chart fixes.

**First impression:** A premium, painterly nature sim: a golden autumn prairie full of real herds, bare trees against a lavender dusk, moonlit snow, a classy serif UI and a chronicle that reads like a documentary narrator. But the tiny brown specks and four floating panels make me doubt I could watch wolves and bison doing anything at the zoom I'd leave it on.

**Scores (1 to 10):** Visual quality 7 / Readability 5 / Sense of life and motion 6 / Ecosystem believability 4 / UI clarity 6 / Would I keep watching 5

## Top issues
1. **Critical: the predators don't really move.** Wolves about 50 and ravens about 300 for 1,400 years; the raven band ruler-straight; wolves 45 to 60 whether bison are at 300 or 4,000; in the drought the ravens crash while wolves barely dip (backwards: scavengers should feast during a die-off); wolves snap back to about 50 after the wipe.
2. **Critical: whole worlds die.** Seed 11 loses all four species by about year 110 with grass at its highest; the shock scenarios persist in 17 to 19 of 20.
3. **Major: evolution reaches the same odd answer everywhere, then stops.** Herding 1.0 to 0.13, sight to 0.63, speed pinned at 1.47, bison and pronghorn litters both at about 2.38 from year 350 to 1,400.
4. **Major: animals don't read as animals at wide zoom.** A dark carpet (01); rust-orange at dusk, brighter than the ground (06); pink confetti from above, with wolves bigger than bison (09); the four-wolf pack one lump (02); wolves a saturated slate-blue like toy plastic.
5. **Major: herd movement looks scripted.** Rings and doughnuts around the northern waterholes with the south half empty (09); long single-file arcs (07, 08).
6. **Major: ground surfaces break the painterly look.** Hard-edged snow blobs like cow print (06, 07); ponds as dark craters or a glowing white disc in a drought (10); the river like asphalt from above (09); low-poly faceted rocks (04, 07, 08); smeared bare slopes (04).
7. **Major: the UI covers the action.** The kill's wolves under the Population panel (03); the trait card a third of the screen (02); the expanded graph (10); the territory legend over a pack label (11); a "Stampede on the plain" banner with no stampede in frame.
8. **Minor: graphs and overlays hard to read.** Stacked y-labels in the expanded graph and unlabeled event diamonds (10); territories as thin low-contrast lines with no visible fill (11); raw trait numbers; species colours differ between the game and the charts; chart 15's subtitle ends in a dangling "commit" and promises red dashed lines and blue bands that aren't there.
9. **Minor: the first hour is spent settling down** (bison 1,100 to 300 in the first decade, ravens 30 to 300).
10. **Minor: the chronicle skips events** (wolves 30 to 39 and ravens 30 to 46 by year 2 with only Year 1 entries shown).

## What's working
1. Light and seasons: the golden autumn opening, bare trees against a lavender dusk, moonlit snow with an iced-over pond, green spring rain; the wind-combed grass at ground level.
2. The chronicle's writing and the Field notes.
3. The follow card: pack, age, founder lineage, current state, condition bars, life story.
4. Real ecological moments: ravens circling a fresh kill with a blood stain; named pack labels that add up to the HUD's wolf count.
5. Prey respond to shocks and persist in 19 of 20 worlds over 1,400 years.

**Single change that would help most:** make wolf and raven numbers depend on prey and carrion so the predators boom and crash a few years behind the herds.
