# Review round 1: blind critic (Opus, fresh, folder path only)

**Scores:** Visual 5 / Readability 3 / Life and motion 5 / Ecosystem believability 2 / UI clarity 6 / Keep watching 3

**First impression:** golden, wind-combed prairie and warm documentary UI look great on a store page; up close the animals are black blobs, nothing visibly happens, and the long-run charts lose predators (or everything) within decades.

Top issues (critic's ranking):
1. Critical: ecosystem doesn't persist (3/16 seeds keep all four species 40 years; every seed opens with a bison boom then crash).
2. Critical: you never see the ecology happen (no chase, kill, carcass, birth, sickness on screen; followed wolf hidden behind a canopy).
3. Major: die-off causes make no ecological sense (herbivores die with grass at its peak; ravens pinned at a hard ~160 ceiling).
4. Major: animals hard to read (bison flat black cutouts; wolves and pronghorn tan specks; nothing visible in wide/top views).
5. Major: half the day/night cycle is unwatchable (near-black night, orange-soup dusk, washed-out top map, exposed map edge).
6. Major: prairie doesn't read as prairie; seasons don't read (orange "midsummer", plow-row stripes, sandy ridges).
7. Major: trees are broccoli blobs; shrubs are bright green sponge balls; canopies block the follow camera.
8. Major: chronicle is spam (repeated stampedes and elders; little predator drama).
9. Minor: water looks like a concrete canal; ponds flat discs.
10. Minor: UI species colors collide; predators flat on the linear graph; unlabeled tool icons; "ticks/s" jargon.

Single change that would help most: keep all four species alive with visible boom-bust in nearly every seed.
