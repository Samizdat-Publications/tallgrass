# Round 4 blind critique (fresh critic, folder path only, 2026-10-03 18:30)

Scores: Visual quality 6 / Readability 4 / Sense of life and motion 5 / Ecosystem believability 5 / UI clarity 6 / Would I keep watching 5.

Top issues (summarized; full text in the session transcript):
1. Critical: animals don't read at the default wide camera (herds as ink smears, pronghorn as specks, wolves invisible); exaggerate silhouettes at range, distinct value per species, marker or outline so wolves are findable.
2. Critical: long-run graphs are fuzzy flat lines; wolves don't respond to prey with a lag; ravens clamped at a ceiling; add multi-year climate variation for long-wavelength stories.
3. Major: evolution anti-ecological (herding collapses to 0.13, sight to 0.63, speed and size plateau at 1.46 in every seed).
4. Major: births in the wrong season (calves in December); add a spring birth pulse and winter mortality.
5. Major: wide and high shots go muddy; hard-edged snow blobs; ponds like shell craters; square world.
6. Major: trees are broccoli lollipops in rows; winter trees are poles; black smudges at branch tips; bushes are blobs.
7. Major: too many animals in too small a valley; herds overlap.
8. Major: shocks don't read as consequences (herd ends up bigger than before; the world is still growing when shocked).
9. Minor: UI: unlabeled tool glyphs, expanded graph leaves wolves and ravens flat in linear, pronghorn green on green grass fill, title cards over the action, chronicle will become a wall, unexplained white rings at wide zoom.
10. Minor: story moments not staged; a followed founder wolf has "nothing to tell yet".

What's working: golden-hour tallgrass close-ups and the cool-grey wolves against gold; the documentary voice (serif, chronicle, title cards); the wolf-removal trophic cascade; persistence (18 to 20 of 20 over 774 years); trait bars with founder and species ticks, and the named-pack territory overlay.

Single change that would help most: make every species instantly readable at the default wide camera through distinct silhouette, value and spacing.
