# Round 32 blind critique (fresh blind-critic, stills of D11 plus the v10b 20-seed overview chart, 2026-10-10 16:05)

**First impression:** The low golden-hour shot of bison and pronghorn among tall grass with sun rays through the trees would make me stop scrolling, and the UI looks like a real nature-journal product. Then the overview shots turn the ecosystem into brown dots packed around a pond on what look like orange sand dunes.

**Scores:** visual 6, readability 4, life and motion 6, ecosystem believability 6, UI 7, keep watching 6.

**Top issues (ranked):**
1. Critical: species cannot be told apart at the default overview distance (01, 11, lk_01, lk_03): bison are brown blobs, pronghorn white flecks like litter, wolves and coyotes indistinguishable; the first-kill toast fires with no visible kill. Fix: distance silhouettes, value contrast against the grass, slight scale exaggeration or a faint species-tinted rim at far zoom.
2. Major: herds packed like a feedlot around one pond (lk_04, lk_08, lk_09, lk_10), every animal in two or three clumps along the creek (lk_03). Fix: spread grazing, stagger water trips, keep the tight ring for drought.
3. Major: bison look plastic up close (lk_05: a glossy burnt-orange airbrushed saddle over the shoulders) and like seals or capybaras at mid range (lk_02 background, lk_06, lk_04). Fix: matte darker coat, cape as a colour shift not a sheen, keep the hump and head silhouette at lower LODs.
4. Major: the prairie reads as a desert of sand dunes at distance (lk_01, lk_03, lk_08, 10); every shot is "Dry years". Fix: far grass colour variation, wind ripples, greener swales, a wet-year look in the showcase.
5. Major: the long-run charts show set points with noise, not lagged boom and bust; mule deer decays about 150 to 30, prairie dogs sink and go extinct in 1 of 20, coyotes ratchet up 50 to 90; raven band 100 to 1,000. Fix: slower larger lagged cycles, stop the slow declines, add a 100-year zoom chart.
6. Major: animals glow at night (lk_09): bison lit pale blue-white like ghosts or sheep while trees and ground are dark; the moonlit pond reflection is blotchy. Fix: keep animals dark with a thin moon rim only; smooth the reflection.
7. Major: birds read as a swarm of flies (245 to 487 ravens as black specks over the upper half of 01, a black clump in 10). Fix: fewer on screen, gather at carcasses, readable flight silhouette.
8. Minor: dust trails fan out as straight radial spokes (01); a flat pink dust decal on a hill (lk_01); small white dash marks over the grass (lk_07); small bushes like green lily pads (lk_08).
9. Minor: pond ice as a uniform white ring like a paper doily (lk_10); dithered noisy edges on autumn crowns (01); hard cut-out leaf-card edges (lk_04, lk_08).
10. Minor UI: idle playback bar nearly invisible (01, 02); "Ridge pack" label faded under the playback bar (11); graph lanes start above zero without saying so, species names appear twice; the hawk lane is an unreadable square wave.

**What's working:** lk_02 is genuinely beautiful (grass blades, sun rays, a horned and humped bison, a pronghorn behind); the wolf chase in 02 (gallop poses, attached shadows) and the animal panel (life story, family tree, traits against founders); the Chronicle and field notes give it a documentary voice; persistence 8 of 9 species in 20 of 20 seeds over 1,200 years; seasonal and atmospheric range.

**The single change that would help most:** make every species recognizable by silhouette and contrast at the default overview distance, and spread the herds across the land instead of piling them around one pond.
