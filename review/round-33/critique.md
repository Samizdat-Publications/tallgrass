# Round 33 blind critique (2026-10-24, pre-polish V1.0 build e7f96e3)

**First impression:** The opening shot sells it: golden aspens, a crescent of bison throwing up a dust plume, wolves on the flank in long morning light. Then you pull back and every animal turns into the same brown speck, the close-ups show bison that look like glossy plastic cattle, and the long-run charts are steady but have very little drama.

**Scores:** Visual quality 6 / Readability 5 / Sense of life and motion 7 / Ecosystem believability 6 / UI clarity 7 / Would I keep watching 6

1. Critical. Species can't be told apart at mid and wide zoom (06, 08, 11); the top map (09) uses coloured chevrons with no legend.
2. Major. Close-up bison look like water buffalo: long forward-curving horns, little hump, wet black-plastic sheen with blue specular highlights, some sunk to the belly, ghosting near x600 y600 (05).
3. Major. Ravens are unlit cut-outs, too big, a stray white stripe; the carcass reads as a dead canid (03).
4. Major. Event callouts promise things the camera isn't showing: "The first kill" (01), "The mousing leap" over pronghorn (04), "Eagles for the winter" over bison (05), a stale "Ride along on a hunt" toast (03).
5. Major. Long-run dynamics stable but flat; prairie dogs and mule deer grind down; coyote median creeps up; the chart says "every scenario" but shows one row (20).
6. Major. Weather has no punch: the blizzard (07) is a clear moonlit night, rain (08) faint streaks, drought (10) ordinary autumn with bluish fog ribbons.
7. Major. Wide terrain looks like a diorama: banded snow like contour lines, a flat grey river, a purple frozen pond, blotchy ground (06, 08, 09, 11).
8. Minor. Bison rise through autumn and winter of year 1; eight or nine cougars beside about 30 mule deer.
9. Minor. The population panel sits on the action and its sparklines are flat in year 1.
10. Minor. Graph axes not starting at zero; "100x at 47x" reads like an apology; den marker against the speed bar; a pack label behind the HUD.

**Working:** the opening aerial (01); the wolf follow-cam and inspector (02); the Chronicle prose and serif title cards; persistence (8 of 9 species 20/20 over 1,200 years); the territory overlay (11); the night palette.

**Single biggest change:** make every species instantly recognisable by silhouette, value and colour at mid and wide zoom.
